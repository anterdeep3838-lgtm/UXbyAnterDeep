11Insights & assumptions

Insights & Assumptions

Four insights. Four moves.

The research pointed to the same truth: players buy for their game. Each insight became one clear design move.

  1. 01

    Players buy for how they play, not for raw numbers.

    Plain-language specsA one-line meaning for every key spec, plus a "Built for" section.

  2. 02

    The choice is made side by side.

    Compare trayAdd from any page, compare up to three, differences highlighted.

  3. 03

    A new season means many items at once.

    Build Your KitA few questions return a full kit with reasons and a live total.

  4. 04

    Trust is won at the finish line.

    Clear checkout & returnsFull cost before payment and returns in three guided steps.

Assumptions I designed around
  • {{i:bolt}}

    Guided beats generic. A kit shaped by sport, level and budget fits better than a fixed bundle.

  • {{i:info}}

    Keep the numbers, add the meaning. A plain-language line sits beside each spec, never instead of it.

  • {{i:arrowL}}

    Visible returns add confidence. Showing the return path early makes buying feel safe.

    14Design iterations

    Design Iterations

    What changed.

    Each revision sharpened what a shopper sees first and what they do next. Flip the switch to see each change.

    1. R1

      Account access

      Account access needed several steps to reach orders and sign out.

      A profile menu gives quick access to My Orders, and to Sign in or Sign out.

    2. R2

      Navigation wording

      Menu labels were generic and did not say where they led.

      Each menu link names the equipment it opens, so the destination is clear.

    3. R3

      Swipe to add

      Add and remove states varied, and quantity sat on the card.

      Add and remove stay consistent every time, and quantity moved to the product page.

    4. R4

      Menus & product cards

      Menus overlapped product cards, and the dropdown felt dated.

      Menus no longer cover product cards, and the dropdown became a modern menu.

    5. R5

      Build Your Kit

      Every question showed the same gear, and the budget question was complex.

      Each question shows different gear, the budget question is simpler, and a finished kit goes to the cart as one bag.

    6. R6

      Focus

      Find Your Size, Gear Care and resistance and training items crowded the store.

      These were removed so the store keeps to its core sports gear.

About me

Clear thinking first. Careful craft all the way through.

I’m Anterdeep Singh, a product designer focused on mobile apps and SaaS products. My expertise spans UX strategy, information architecture, interaction design, UI design and prototyping.

I combine user research with product thinking to uncover friction, challenge assumptions and shape practical solutions. I pay particular attention to unclear choices, unnecessary steps and the edge cases that can break an otherwise good experience.

I work with product and engineering to create accessible, consistent experiences that balance user needs, business goals and technical constraints.

The goal is simple: help people spend less time figuring out the interface and more time doing what they came for.

How I approach UX

I start by asking where people hesitate, what they misunderstand and what makes a task unnecessarily difficult. I combine user research with product thinking to define the problem and weigh trade-offs across user needs, business goals and technical constraints. Through information architecture, interaction design and prototyping, I explore clearer paths with accessibility and edge cases considered from the start.

Where AI fits in my workflow

I use AI to shorten the distance between an idea and something I can evaluate. It supports research synthesis, ideation and rapid prototyping, helping me explore alternatives and identify potential gaps in user flows. I check its outputs against source material, user needs and technical constraints. AI suggestions remain hypotheses until validated; I take responsibility for the final design decisions.

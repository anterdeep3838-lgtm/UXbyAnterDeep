In a month of NovaWear support, AI resolves most conversations, and more than a third still need a person. Both sides need great design.

(10) — Strategy & scopeResolveAI
10Product strategy& scope

One principle decided what went into the first release, and what stayed out on purpose.

Principle

Keep context connected, automate within clear limits, and let humans take control when needed.

Problems, in priority order
  1. Context lost at handoffThe biggest pain in the research
  2. Channels in silosOne customer, many threads
  3. AI without limitsRefunds and accounts need people
First release
  • Unified Inbox for six channels
  • AI replies with a confidence rating
  • Handoff summary, Pick up and Take over
  • Escalation and refund approval
  • Knowledge Base and automations
  • Overview, Analytics and the chat widget
  • Sign-up and 7-step onboarding
Left out on purpose
  • AI that moves moneyAI prepares refunds; only a person approves
  • Voice callsResolveAI covers chat, messaging and email
  • A separate agent appThe web app already works down to phone size

Designed so that
  • Customers never repeat themselves. The summary and order travel with the thread.
  • Agents pick up in seconds. Six fields replace a long transcript.
  • Leads trust the AI. Its limits are written in plain words.
What I learned
  • Design the handoff first. The moment AI steps back decides how the whole product feels.
  • Plain words build trust. Rules anyone can read beat clever settings.
  • Write the rules down. A clear spec kept an AI-assisted build consistent.

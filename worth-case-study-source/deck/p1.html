Worth02 · The problem

Problem& Solution

Worth makes everyday money easy to record, easy to understand and easy to keep within limits.

❯❯❯❯

The problem

Money leaves in many small UPI payments. Logging them feels like a chore, budgets are noticed only after they break, and nobody explains where the money went.

Solution 1 · Record in seconds

A central Add with a calculator keypad, or just say it: voice entry records expenses, income and savings, then waits for a quick review.

Solution 2 · Guided, not surprised

Category budgets alert before a limit is reached, Analysis shows where money went, and Finly AI answers questions about your own spending.

Worth02 · Problem framing

4Ws + H

Five questions that shaped the brief.

How might we

help people stay in control of everyday money without the effort of a spreadsheet?

  1. Who

    Salaried young professionals in India, roughly 22–35, who pay digitally many times a day.

  2. What

    Logging feels tedious, budgets get checked too late, and spending patterns stay unclear.

  3. Where

    In everyday moments: right after a payment, before a purchase, and when the month closes.

  4. Why

    Without real-time feedback, budgets go unchecked and savings goals slip (CFPB, 2017).

  5. How

    Tap or voice entry, category budgets with early alerts, and Analysis plus Finly AI to explain the numbers.

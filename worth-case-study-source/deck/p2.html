Worth05 · Success criteria

05 · Success criteria

Four outcomes that
define success.

Outcome 01

Log in seconds

Recording an expense, income or saving takes a few taps, or one spoken sentence.

Goal: under 15 seconds per entry

Outcome 02

No budget surprises

People hear about a budget while there is still time to act, not after it breaks.

Goal: an alert at 70, 80 or 90% of every limit

Outcome 03

Spending that makes sense

Anyone can name their top category and how it compares with last month.

Goal: answered from one Analysis screen

Outcome 04

Answers on demand

Questions like “Why did my expenses rise?” get a clear answer from Finly.

Goal: every answer built from the user’s own data

Worth07 · Product evolution

Product Evolution

SpendWise → Worth.

SpendWise worked, but every element spoke at the same volume. Worth gives the answer room and lets the rest support it.

SpendWise · v1Worth · v2

  1. Money Left card

    BeforeA big number and an unlabelled lime bar.

    AfterThe period is named, the bar says “Spent 65%”, and the amount can be hidden.

    Why: a number only helps when you know what it covers.

  2. One accent colour

    BeforePurple and lime competed for attention.

    AfterOne blue for actions; mint and coral only mean income and spending.

    Why: colour should carry meaning, not decoration.

  3. Floating dock

    BeforeA fixed tab bar with a raised + button.

    AfterA slim dock that names the active tab, with Add in the centre.

    Why: more room for content, and Add is always within reach.

  4. Finly insight

    BeforeA one-line tip, cut off mid-sentence.

    AfterA full insight that says it's “based on your transactions”, plus Ask Finly.

    Why: advice is trusted when its source is clear.

Worth07 · Product evolution

What Changed Across the App

What changed across the app.

Five areas, each moved from showing everything at once to showing the next useful thing.

  1. Add transaction

    One long form: amount, eight categories, description, date, payment, note and repeat, all on one page.

    Two calm steps. Amount first on a calculator keypad, then details. Voice entry shows what it heard before saving.

  2. Analysis

    Three stat tiles, a donut and weekly bars, each telling part of the story.

    One answer first. What you spent, the daily average and a fair comparison with the same days last period.

  3. Budget

    Edit and delete icons on every category row.

    Status and pace. An overall gauge, spend per day and clear labels; changes live behind “Edit limit”.

  4. Savings

    Rings with saved, target and remaining for each goal.

    Goals become actions. “Save ₹3,500 a month to finish on time”, right under the progress bar.

  5. Navigation

    A single letter avatar in the corner of Home.

    Everything one tap away. The avatar opens a side menu with Profile, Currency, AI preferences and Security.

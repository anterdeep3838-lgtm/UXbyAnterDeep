Worth11 · User control

11 · User control

Nothing is saved
until you check it.

Say “Spent about 2k at Phoenix mall” and Worth shows what it understood first. Voice uses the phone's speech recognition; typing works the same way.

    What it heard, and each field it filled in.

  • Unsure fields flagged

    “Change” returns to the amount; every field stays editable.
